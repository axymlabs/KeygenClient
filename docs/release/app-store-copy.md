Name: **Keygen Client** (check availability and rights before registering)

Subtitle: **Licensing, connected**

Category: Developer Tools

Promotional text:

Keep your licensing close. Connect to Keygen Cloud or your self-hosted server to manage licences, policies, products, machines and users.

Description:

Your licences. In hand.

Keygen Client brings your Keygen licensing server to your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Connect with your admin account or API token and work with the resources your account can access.

• Review licensing activity from your dashboard.
• Browse and manage licences, policies and products.
• Work with machines and users.
• Create reusable licence-file formats.
• Share licence files and QR codes, or check out signed files when your policy supports signing.
• Use a responsive interface with light and dark appearances.

Requires an existing Keygen Cloud or self-hosted Keygen account and an accessible server. Available actions depend on your account permissions and server configuration. An internet or local network connection to your server is required.

An independent client; not affiliated with or endorsed by Keygen.
